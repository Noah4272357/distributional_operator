**Table X. Validation performance on the inverse Burgers distribution-learning task.** Models infer the distribution of 256-dimensional initial conditions from the corresponding solution distribution. Each experiment used 1,000 training and 200 validation distribution pairs from the 1,400-pair dataset; lower values are better for all metrics. Sinkhorn denotes the debiased Sinkhorn divergence with transport exponent (p=2), blur (0.05), and scaling (0.5); MMD denotes the biased radial-basis-function maximum mean discrepancy with a per-batch median-heuristic bandwidth; SW2 denotes the sliced Wasserstein-2 distance computed with 128 random projections; and Energy denotes the empirical energy distance. Conditional-flow entries are the mean (\pm) sample standard deviation over five 1,000-epoch runs with random seeds 0--4, using the metrics from the epoch attaining the lowest validation Sinkhorn divergence in each run. The kernel-regression entry is from one run with seed 0 and bandwidth (0.005), selected by minimum validation Sinkhorn divergence over the configured bandwidth grid; therefore, no uncertainty estimate is reported for this model. The conditional flow used all 200 samples per distribution, whereas kernel regression used a fixed random subset of 64 samples per distribution.
